# Round 84 example: county disability prevalence and poverty (MI and NY, 2019-2023 ACS)

Exploratory; author self-review, not independent. Ecologic and cross-sectional: describes counties, not people.

145 counties; overall disability prevalence 12.9%. Poverty 20th percentile 10.5%, 80th 15.6%.

| Measure | Estimate | 90% CI |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Marginal risk at poverty 10.5% | 12.04% | |
| Marginal risk at poverty 15.6% | 13.39% | |
| Risk ratio (80th vs 20th) | 1.112 | 1.065 to 1.162 |
| Risk difference (points) | 1.35 | 0.76 to 1.94 |
| Attributable fraction (all counties at the 20th percentile) | 0.064 | 0.036 to 0.091 |

Dose-response over poverty quintiles: marginal risks 11.06%, 12.60%, 12.94%, 14.30%, 14.03%; linear trend p = 0.00509; departure from linearity p = 0.806.

The prevalence of disability among counties at the 80th poverty percentile was 1.11 times as high as among counties at the 20th percentile (an association between areas).
