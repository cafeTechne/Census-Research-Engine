# Round 99 example: Medicaid expansion as a triple difference, and matching (exploratory)

Author self-review, not independent; not a registered study.

**Triple difference** (ACS 1-year B27010, 2010-2019; 26 states expanding in 2014 vs 12 not expanded by 2019; adults 19-64 vs 65+): -1.76 points of uninsurance (90% CI -3.75 to 0.22; 38 states clustered; validated). Round 78's staggered DiD on all-age uninsurance found -1.23 (-2.18 to -0.27); the 65+ group absorbs state-specific shocks common to both age groups.

**Matching, metro status and county uninsured % (MI/NY/OH/PA, 2019-2023):** nearest-neighbour ATE -1.33 (-2.22 to -0.45); coarsened exact matching ATT -2.14 (-4.37 to 0.09; 100% of metro counties matched). Round 90's overlap-weighted estimate was -1.66.
