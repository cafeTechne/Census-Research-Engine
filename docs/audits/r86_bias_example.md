# Round 86 example: bias analysis of the Michigan uninsured comparison (PUMS 2019-2023, adults 19-64)

Exploratory; author self-review, not independent.

Crude risk ratio of uninsurance, Black vs White non-Hispanic adults: 1.53 (90% CI 1.46 to 1.60).

E-value: 2.43 (interval limit 2.27): an unmeasured confounder would need risk-ratio associations of at least 2.43 with both race and uninsurance to explain the crude ratio away. (Round 79 found the gap is explained by measured composition, so this crude ratio is not a causal effect of race in any case.)

Outcome misclassification with ILLUSTRATIVE values (sensitivity .85, specificity .97 for reporting uninsurance; not taken from a validation study): corrected RR 1.97 (observed 1.53); probabilistic analysis (exploratory; failed validation) median 1.71, 90% simulation interval 1.53 to 4.47.
